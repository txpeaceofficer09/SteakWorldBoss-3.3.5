# SteakWorldBoss
### World of Warcraft: Wrath of the Lich King 3.3.5a

![Screenshot of SteakWorldBoss AddOn](https://trap-nine.com/screenshots/WoWScrnShot_031126_093813.jpg)

The addon places a small movable countdown timer on screen.  Clicking the countdown will post a message to raid (if you are in one), party (if you are not in a raid, but in a party) or guild (if you are not in a group).  It also puts up a toast message 60 minutes, 30 minutes, 10 minutes and at the boss spawn.
